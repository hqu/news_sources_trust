Who the gap belongs to

The same survey, asked as a difference between people rather than a coefficient. Pick any two groups — Democrats and Republicans, graduates and non-graduates, or people who use a news source and people who don't — and see the share of each who hold a belief or trust an institution, and how far apart they are in percentage points. A difference of 31 points between two groups is a sentence anyone can read; an odds ratio of 3.9 is not.
